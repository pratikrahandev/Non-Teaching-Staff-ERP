
# Non-Teaching Staff ERP

**Non-Teaching Staff ERP** is a staff administration and management system developed by **Pratik Sanjay Rahane**.

The system is designed to digitize and simplify the management of non-teaching staff and administrative operations.

## Key Features

- Staff Management
- Attendance Management
- Leave Management
- Payroll Management
- Document Management
- Notices & Announcements
- Staff Records
- Reports & Administration
